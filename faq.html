Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about working with our team.

What services does Small Business Dreambuilders offer?

We offer business consulting, business formation, business plan development, financial management and reporting, QuickBooks training, tax planning and preparation, government contracting support, project management, workforce development, and grant writing services. See our full services list for details.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in and primarily serve small businesses throughout Hennepin County, Minnesota, and are recognized as an Elevate Hennepin business advisor.

Do you offer a free consultation?

Yes. Reach out through our contact page or call us at (612) 466-0131 to schedule a free initial consultation and talk through your business needs.

I'm just starting out — can you still help me?

Absolutely. We work with entrepreneurs at every stage, from initial business formation and business plan development through to established businesses looking to grow, manage finances, or pursue government contracts.

Can you help with QuickBooks and financial reporting?

Yes. We offer hands-on QuickBooks training as well as ongoing financial management, projections and analysis, and financial reporting support.

Do you help with grant writing?

Yes, our writing services include grant writing and other technical documents to help your business pursue funding opportunities.

How do I get started?

Call us at (612) 466-0131 or send a message through our contact form, and we'll follow up to schedule your consultation.
